# JobFinder Product Requirements

> Status: **Draft — not yet written.**

The V1 product focuses on connecting job seekers with employers in the
Ethiopian market through:

- A mobile application for job seekers
- Employer and admin web dashboards

Roles:

- `JOB_SEEKER`
- `EMPLOYER`
- `ADMIN`

Detailed product requirements are not yet documented. They will be added
